Essential Home Vietnam

Established in March 2019, Essential Home Vietnam manufactures casegoods and upholstered furniture. Its Vietnam operations bring together production facilities, offices and warehousing, with expansion recorded in 2022.

EH Vietnam Capabilities

Essential Home Vietnam brings together casegoods and upholstery manufacturing with sample development and a range of production processes. Material preparation, CNC machining and furniture assembly work alongside hand sanding, manual finishing and automated finishing. These capabilities connect the development of individual components with the construction and surface detail of the finished piece.
