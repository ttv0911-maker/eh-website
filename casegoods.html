Our Capabilities

CASEGOODS

Made For Different Ways Of Living.

Casegoods form the foundation of EH Group’s manufacturing experience.
Our work spans residential furniture and specialized categories, including
bathroom furniture, kitchen accents and hospitality furniture.

Hand sanding, manual finishing and automated finishing contribute to the final appearance of each piece.

Craftsmanship & Technology

Old World Meets New World.

From material preparation to final inspection, our production combines CNC precision with hands-on craftsmanship. Machine technicians work alongside hand-carving specialists, bringing efficiency and a human touch to every stage.

Precision In Production

Lumber inspection, rough milling and CNC machining prepare materials and components for furniture assembly.

Character In The Details

Hand-carving and hand sanding bring craftsmanship to the surface, shaping decorative details and preparing each piece for finishing.

From Surface To Finished Piece

Manual and automated finishing lines are followed by drying, final assembly and quality inspection before packaging and shipping preparation.
