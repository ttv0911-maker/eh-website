Our Capabilities

Upholstery

Upholstered furniture that brings together form, comfort & craftsmanship for residential and commercial interiors.

Product Development

From initial ideas to samples, we work with you to develop furniture
around your design and project requirements.

01.

Project Review

We review the customer’s concept, drawings, specifications, target pricing and project requirements to assess feasibility and identify any information that needs clarification.

02.

Design and Engineering

Our team supports drawing development and review, product construction, dimensions and manufacturing feasibility. We may recommend practical adjustments to improve functionality, production efficiency and cost effectiveness.

03.

Material and Finish Selection

We review suitable materials, hardware, finishes and components based on the project requirements. Material samples, finish swatches and alternative options can be provided for customer review where required.

04.

Sample Development

After the drawings and key specifications are confirmed, we develop samples for evaluation. The schedule depends on product complexity, material availability, finishing requirements and component lead times.

05.

Sample Review and Approval

We review customer feedback, make necessary revisions and submit updated samples or specifications for approval. Bulk production proceeds after the required sample, specifications and other applicable approvals are confirmed.

Materials & Finishes

Manual & automated finishing processes bring out the character of each design, with wood tones, metallic details and matte or gloss surfaces.

Our furniture combines solid wood, veneers and engineered wood with natural stone and solid-surface elements, bringing varied textures and character to each piece.

Sourcing

Material sourcing support, including customer-specified wood veneers, to meet the needs of your furniture program.

Quality

Materials, manufacturing stages, finished products and packaging are checked against approved specifications and the agreed inspection plan. Corrective actions and re-inspection address identified issues.

How We Work

Share Your Project Requirements

Provide your drawings, reference images or samples, material and finish specifications, estimated quantities, target pricing and required dates. Please include packaging, testing and compliance requirements so we can assess the project.

Confirm OEM or ODM Collaboration

For OEM projects, customers generally provide designs and specifications while EH supports manufacturing engineering, sourcing, samples, production and quality control. For ODM projects, EH provides additional development support, including construction, material and finish recommendations.

Review Drawings and Samples

Review product drawings, dimensions, construction details, materials, hardware, finishes and colors. Sample development follows confirmation of the drawings and key specifications; customer feedback is reviewed and revisions are submitted for approval.

Prepare for Shipment

Products and packaging are checked before shipment against the agreed inspection plan and customer requirements. Bulk production typically requires approximately 90–120 days after PPS approval and completion of required testing and specification sign-off; the final schedule depends on the project.
