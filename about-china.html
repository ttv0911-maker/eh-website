Essential Home China combines casegoods and upholstery manufacturing, building on the Group’s furniture production roots in China. Its operations support furniture for residential and commercial applications, bringing together wood furniture construction and upholstered elements. Based in Jiujiang, the company also serves as the Group’s China corporate office.

With Roots In Furniture Manufacturing Since 2013, EH Group Brings Together Three Companies Across Vietnam And China. Our Work Spans Casegoods And Upholstery, Combining Craftsmanship And Technology To Develop And Manufacture Furniture.
