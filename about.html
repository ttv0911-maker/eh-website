About EH Group

With Roots In Furniture Manufacturing Since 2013, EH Group Brings Together Essential Home Vietnam, Essential Home China And 99 (Ninety Nine), Across Vietnam And China. Our Work Spans Casegoods And Upholstery, Supported By Product Development, Craftsmanship And Wood Veneer Sourcing.

Quality

Attention to materials, finishing and the details of every completed piece.

Craftsmanship

The human touch, supported by technology, from construction to the final finish.

Cooperation

Working with you to develop furniture around your design and project requirements.

Mission & Vision

We bring our partners’ furniture ideas to life with thoughtful development, skilled craftsmanship, and dependable manufacturing. From the first concept to the finished piece, we work closely together to preserve the design intent and deliver quality in every detail.

We aspire to be a trusted manufacturing partner for furniture brands around the world. By growing our expertise and building lasting relationships, we aim to help our partners move forward with confidence, collection after collection.

“We believe in honest partnerships, thoughtful craftsmanship, and quality that lasts—values that guide every piece we make and every relationship we build.”
