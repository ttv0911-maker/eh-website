About EH Group

With Roots In Furniture Manufacturing Since 2013, EH Group Brings Together Essential Home Vietnam, Essential Home China And 99 (Ninety Nine), Across Vietnam And China. Our Work Spans Casegoods And Upholstery, Supported By Product Development, Craftsmanship And Wood Veneer Sourcing.

2013201920202021202220242025
2013

Our first chapter in China

Founded in July 2013 as Furnline Furniture Factory Company Ltd, our original company began in Liangjing, near Huizhou, China. Specializing in casegoods for residential and commercial spaces, it laid the foundation for EH Group.

2019

Growing into Vietnam

Essential Home Vietnam opened in March 2019 in Ben Cat, Binh Duong, bringing manufacturing, offices and warehousing together. That same year, the original China factory relocated to a larger facility in Huizhou.

Explore EH Vietnam ↗
2020

Establishing Essential Home in Jiujiang

Essential Home Furniture was established in Jiujiang, China, marking a new chapter in the Group’s manufacturing presence. Across 2019–2020, the business also expanded into upholstered furniture.

Explore EH China ↗
2021

A stronger material supply chain

Ninety-Nine Furniture was established in Ben Cat, Vietnam, to address supply-chain challenges during Covid. Its initial focus was sourcing and holding inventory of premium wood veneers specified by customers.

Explore 99 ↗
2022

Expanding our manufacturing capabilities

In late 2024, the Ninety-Nine facilities doubled in size, expanding the company’s physical footprint within the Group’s Vietnam operations.

2024

More room for growth

In late 2024, the Ninety-Nine facilities doubled in size, expanding the company’s footprint in Ben Cat and supporting its growing operations.

2025

Planning the next chapter in Jiujiang

The Group outlined plans to consolidate its China operations into a larger facility in Jiujiang, aimed at improving efficiency and supporting faster product development.
