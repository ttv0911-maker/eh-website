Process

01.

Tell Us What You
Have In Mind.

  • Product categories and intended market
  • Drawings, concepts or reference images
  • Target quantities
  • Preferred timing
  • Key project priorities

Share where your project stands, what you want to achieve and the support you need. Initial ideas and detailed briefs are both useful starting points.

02.

Establish A
Shared Direction.

We discuss the scope, product requirements and timing with you. This conversation helps identify what is ready to move forward and what needs further clarification.

03.

Make Decisions
With A Clear
Reference.

Review the drawings, material references, finish samples or product samples relevant to your project. Share consolidated feedback so revisions and outstanding decisions can be addressed together.

04.

Agree On What
Will Be Made.

Confirm the sample, specifications, quantities and agreed project details before manufacturing. These approvals establish a shared reference for the next stage.

05.

Plan For The
Final Handover.

Discuss inspection requirements, packaging needs and shipping arrangements with our team so preparation reflects your project requirements.
